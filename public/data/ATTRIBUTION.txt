Experimental data accompanying Application of multiplet structure deconvolution
to extract scalar coupling constants from 1D NMR spectra, Damien Jeannerat and
Carlos Cobas. Files are unmodified. Display normalization, reference shifts,
and baseline correction are local viewing operations and do not alter these files.

No MRI reference volumes are bundled. The MRI workspace displays only scans that
the user imports or opens from a public repository.
